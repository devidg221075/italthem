## TITLE
VACUUM FLAKE PRODUCTION SYSTEM IT.FM
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ITALTHERM Metal Flake Making Machine is specially designed to make gold and silver dry and thin flakes, which greatly improves refining efficiency.
After melting, the material is directly formed into thin flakes ≤0.1mm in size, these thin flaks dissolve rapidly and completely in the chemical reagents Sealed operation and inert gas protection isolate oxygen throughout the process, preventing high-temperature oxidation.
