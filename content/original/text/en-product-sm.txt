## TITLE
STATIC METAL MELTING FURNACE IT.SM
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ITALTHERM Static metal melting furnace is a compact and efficient solution for melting precious metals such as gold, silver, with capacities ranging from 1 kg to 15 kg.
Equipped with advanced electromagnetic induction heating technology, the furnace can reach melting temperatures within 3–5 minutes, allowing efficient melting and homogeneous alloys
Equipped with K type immersion thermocouple, gas protection torches over the crucible and the removal pincers.
