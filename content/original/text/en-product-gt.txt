## TITLE
GEAR-DRIVEN TILTING METAL MELTING FURNACE MODEL IT.GT
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ItalTherm Gear-Driven Tilting Metal Melting Furnace delivers robust capabilities for melting precious metals such as gold, silver, with capacities ranging from 20 kg to 50 kg.
Equipped with energy-efficient medium frequency induction power generator and precise temperature control for rapid and uniform heating.
The tilting mechanism is driven by a cost-effective gear reduction drive system with smooth controlled tilting and precise Curved Pouring Trajectory.
