## TITLE
CONTROLLED ATMOSPHERE BELT FURNACE MODEL IT.BF
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ItalTherm Mesh Belt Heat Treatment Furnace.
A continuous‑process furnace for welding, brazing, sintering, and annealing metal components. The mesh‑belt system ensures uninterrupted production, while an inert‑gas atmosphere and oxygen‑burning barrier prevent oxidation. The furnace features programmable PID temperature control, independent heating zones, and a controlled cooling section for consistent, high‑quality results across a wide range of applications.
