ItalTherm Mesh Belt Heat Treatment Furnace
The ItalTherm mesh belt heat treatment furnace is designed for continuous welding, brazing, sintering, and annealing of metal components, including jewelry and precision parts. The mesh‑belt transport system enables uninterrupted processing, while the entire cycle takes place under inert‑gas protection to prevent oxidation.
An oxygen‑burning barrier at the furnace entrance eliminates residual oxygen, ensuring a clean, controlled atmosphere. After heating, workpieces move directly into the cooling zone for stable, repeatable results.
The system can be equipped with PID temperature control, allowing programmable heating curves, soaking times, and cooling profiles for full automation.
Key Features
Mesh Belt Transport System
High‑temperature‑resistant metal belt (stainless steel or nickel alloys)
Adjustable speed from 0.1 to 5 m/min for different processes
Furnace Zones
Preheating zone to avoid thermal shock
Heating zone with temperatures from 300°C to 1200°C
Cooling zone with controlled air or water cooling
Heating Technology
Electric resistance, SiC rods, gas, or infrared heating
Independently controlled temperature zones with ±3°C uniformity
Protective Atmosphere
Nitrogen, hydrogen, or mixed gases to prevent oxidation and ensure clean joints
Automation & Control
PLC or industrial computer for temperature, belt speed, and gas‑flow management
Typical Applications
Electronics: SMT soldering, LED packaging
Metalworking: brazing of stainless steel/aluminum, powder metallurgy sintering
Automotive: sensors, motor components, battery tabs
Photovoltaics: solar cell string soldering
Core Advantages
Continuous 24/7 production for high‑volume output
Precise control of heating curves and cooling profiles
Energy‑efficient closed design with compliant exhaust treatment
Adaptable to various alloys and solder types
