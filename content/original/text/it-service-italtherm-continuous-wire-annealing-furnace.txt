ItalTherm Continuous Wire Annealing Furnace
## BODY
ItalTherm Continuous Wire Annealing Furnace
A high‑precision system engineered for the continuous annealing of precious‑metal wires, delivering exceptional temperature stability, metallurgical consistency, and bright‑surface results.
Key Features
Precision Heating Process
The furnace uses an advanced resistance‑heating system to ensure highly uniform temperature distribution along the entire heating chamber.
Adjustable annealing range: 300°C to 800°C
Touchscreen HMI for programming custom temperature profiles based on wire alloy, diameter, and production speed.
Controlled Soaking Stage
Once the target temperature is reached, the furnace maintains a stable soaking period to guarantee full recrystallization.
Eliminates drawing‑induced internal stresses
Enhances ductility, grain uniformity, and overall wire consistency
Rapid Water-Cooling System
An integrated quench tank provides immediate water cooling after annealing.
Refines grain structure
Improves mechanical properties
Ensures stable, repeatable metallurgical results
Servo‑Controlled Wire Tension System
High‑precision servo motors automatically regulate wire tension throughout the process.
Prevents tangling and deformation
Ensures smooth handling during annealing and coiling
Essential for ultra‑fine wires where tension stability is critical
Hydrogen‑Rich Protective Atmosphere
The furnace operates with controlled protection gases, including hydrogen‑rich atmospheres.
Minimizes surface oxidation
Produces bright, clean, oxide‑free wire
Ideal for gold, silver, platinum, and specialty alloys
