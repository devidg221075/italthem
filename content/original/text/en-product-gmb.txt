## TITLE
GRAINS PRODUCTION SYSTEM MODEL IT.GMB
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ItalTherm AUTOMATIC GRAINS MELTING SYSTEM equipped with advanced medium-frequency induction melting technology. Designed for Alloys and Oxidation-Sensitive Metals. It ensures rapid and uniform heating with precise temperature control necessary for maintaining metal integrity and fluidity.
The whole granulation process and the grains cooling, offloading and draining are fully Automatic to grant operation efficiency and safety.
The granulation process is protected by inert gas to minimize the oxidation of metals.
