## TITLE
MANUAL TILTING METAL MELTING FURNACE MODEL IT.MT
## SHORT DESCRIPTION
ItalTherm Manual tilting metal melting furnace is a compact and efficient solution for melting precious metals such as gold, silver, with capacities ranging from 1 kg to 15 kg.
Equipped with advanced electromagnetic induction heating technology, the furnace can reach melting temperatures within 3–5 minutes, allowing efficient melting and homogeneous alloys.
Its fully integrated design, combining the tilting mechanism and power supply into a single unit, ensures easy and convenient pouring operation and minimizes risks and space requirements.
